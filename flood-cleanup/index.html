In nearly every case the water falls into Category 2, known as gray water, or Category 3, known as black water, which means contaminants, bacteria and other hazards that are a real health risk. East Los Angeles Restoration handles emergency flood cleanup across East Los Angeles and answers 24/7 to pump out the water, sanitize what it touched and restore your property safely.

The first job is getting the water out quickly, and our submersible and truck-mounted pumps are able to clear thousands of gallons every hour. After that, IICRC-certified technicians on our team measure how contaminated the area is, then sanitize it with antimicrobial products, HEPA-filtered air cleaning and targeted disinfection of all surfaces the water reached. Materials beyond saving, like contaminated drywall, damaged insulation and saturated carpet pad, are removed and disposed of the right way.

Once the space is extracted and sanitized, our team brings in commercial drying gear, namely low-grain refrigerant (LGR) dehumidifiers plus high-velocity air movers, so the structural components dry all the way through. Every stage of the job is recorded for your claim: photographs, detailed moisture maps, logs of the equipment used and the scope-of-work reports that insurance adjusters require.

Signs You Need Flood Cleanup in East Los Angeles

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Tide marks on drywall or trim where the water peaked
  • A sump pump that failed during heavy rain and let the basement flood
  • Appliances such as a water heater, washer or dishwasher spilling water across the floor
  • Receding water that leaves muddy residue and debris behind
  • Rainwater getting inside through a roof or wall damaged by a storm
  • Ground so saturated that puddles sit against the foundation walls
  • Seepage at door thresholds, window wells or cracks in the foundation

Why Hire a Professional for Flood Cleanup?

There is much more to a flood cleanup than removing the water you can see. Chemicals, bacteria, sewage and other contamination ride in with the flood and create real health dangers. Protecting your family from E. coli, hepatitis and similar waterborne illness takes proper containment, correct use of protective equipment (PPE) and proven sanitizing methods, all things professional teams train for.

Beyond the health concerns, a poor flood cleanup often leaves hidden moisture that turns into severe mold growth within days. Professional technicians use moisture meters, thermal imaging and psychrometric calculations to make sure every affected area, including wall cavities, subfloors and framing, reaches an acceptable drying standard. Nothing else reliably heads off secondary damage, and that damage can run thousands more to remediate, so we dry by the numbers.

Maintenance Tips for Flood Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • In areas of the property that tend to flood, raise electrical systems and appliances off the floor
  • Keep gutters and storm drains clear of debris so water can flow properly
  • Consider flood insurance even if you are not in a designated flood zone
  • Check that grading on all sides of the house carries water away from the foundation
  • Keep documents and keepsakes in watertight bins on shelves, not on a basement floor
  • A battery-powered backup sump pump keeps water moving if the electricity fails in a storm
